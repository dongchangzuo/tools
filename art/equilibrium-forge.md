# Equilibrium Forge（均衡锻造）

## Algorithmic Philosophy

Equilibrium Forge treats the balance scale not as an illustration but as a **living mechanical system** whose appearance emerges from layered computation. Every surface is a field: wood grain flows along anisotropic noise; brushed metal carries directional micro-scratches; frosted matte zones diffuse light through low-contrast gradients and sparse speckle. The algorithm does not paste textures—it **grows** them from seeded hash noise, sine fibers, and gradient calculus, as though a craftsman had spent countless hours calibrating each octave and highlight band.

Structure unfolds in **geometric strata**. A trapezoidal base anchors the composition; a pillar rises with procedurally engraved ticks—short line segments whose spacing follows a rational scale, echoing measurement without typographic assets. Above the knife-edge pivot, the beam rotates as a rigid body while pans remain world-horizontal, suspended by vertical hooks that obey gravity in screen space. This separation of coordinate systems is the computational expression of torque balance: rotation confined to the beam, stability imposed on the loads.

**Light is sculpted, not painted.** A fixed primary direction casts beveled edges, specular ribbons on metal, and ambient occlusion strips where planes meet. Radial highlights on the pivot cap and elliptical contact shadows on the ground are pure gradient math—products of painstaking tuning where every stop was chosen to sell volume without photographic input. The final image must read as the work of someone at the absolute top of procedural rendering craft.

**Motion completes the metaphor.** When imbalance arrives, angular momentum does not teleport; a damped spring integrates toward the target tilt, permitting slight overshoot and decay—the pendulum logic of a real teaching balance. Subtle temporal wobble on tray contents suggests mass without breaking horizontal truth. The equals sign may fracture, but the forge’s underlying order remains: parameters, seeds, and forces reproducible to the last pixel.

Beauty lives in the **process**: each frame is deterministic given seed and state; each imbalance is a new equilibrium sought. Equilibrium Forge is meticulous algorithmic expression—emergent stillness after controlled chaos, refined through deep computational expertise until the machine itself seems to weigh the world.
